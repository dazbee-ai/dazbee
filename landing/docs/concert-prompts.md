# Concert stadium: layer prompts

One camera for every layer: standing in the middle of the crowd 40 m from the stage, eye height 2 m, looking dead straight at it, 35 mm lens, vanishing point at the centre 40% down. Layers stack back to front in this order: backdrop, stage (ads go into its black screens), moving heads along the truss, band, haze, barrier, crowd far, crowd mid, crowd near, phones up close. Light is neutral white in all of them; the route colours are added in code.

Save each one into `art/concert/stadium/` with the file name given.
