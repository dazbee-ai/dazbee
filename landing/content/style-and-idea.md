# Style and idea

Proposed 2026-11-18, waiting for Yuvaraj's OK. Builds on the moodboard (design-board) and the Dazbee design system.

## Big idea
A playful retro desktop. The agency's brief is a small text file; opened in Dazbee, it pops out three windows, each a different concept with its own colour and angle, each fanning into four ad sizes. One brief, genuinely different ideas, not copies. Approvals show a small pixel "Approved" level-up toast.

## Style
The louder sibling of the app: same shapes and fonts, turned up.
- Ground: cream #f4f1ea with the faint 40px graph grid.
- Colour: ink text and edges; yellow for the one main button; pink only for AI; red, blue, green one per concept window; grey only for look-alike copies.
- Type: Archivo headlines, Figtree body, JetBrains Mono numbers. Sentence case.
- Shapes: 2–3px ink edges, 4–6px hard offset shadow, 10–14px corners, window title bars with three dots, one button shape.
- Texture: light riso grain on illustrations only.
- Pixel art: only the Approved toast and a footer scenery band.
- Motion: one ease-out, 140–300ms pop-ins, no bounce, still under reduced motion.
- Light first; dark later.

## Section looks
1. Hero: headline left; desktop scene right (brief file opens, three concept windows pop out).
2. Example: the scene laid flat: brief left, three concept rows right, four sizes each, angle stickers.
3. Problem: pile of ten grey near-identical cards fading, beside three bright windows.
4. Try it: one big "new-brief.txt" window; results return as three concept windows.
5. How it works: three window cards with boxed mono digits.
6. Pilot: wide yellow band, five boxed slots. Footer: pixel scenery band.

## Don't
No mascot or bee, no purple, no stock photos or 3D for now, no gradients, no tilted buttons, no real brand names in examples, no copying moodboard art.
