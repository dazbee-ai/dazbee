      Questions teams ask.

      Does Dazbee write the strategy?

      No. Your planners' brief and idea lead. Dazbee builds routes from them and keeps each one on brief.

      What counts as a distinct route?

      A different angle on the proposition, such as proof, routine or ingredient. Not the same layout in new colours.

      How many routes can we make?

      As many as the brief calls for. Each one is a different angle, not a recolour of the last.

      Which sizes come out?

      Square 1:1, portrait 4:5, story 9:16 and landscape 1.91:1, the four Meta placements.

      Can we change everything?

      Yes. Every banner is built from layers: headline, images, logo, colours and button.

      What does the pilot cost?

      Nothing. We want five agencies using it on live briefs and telling us what to fix.

      We're a brand, not an agency. Can we try it?

      Yes. The try-out works for anyone with a brief, and the pilot has room for in-house teams that run their own Meta ads.

      Does it publish to Meta?

      Not yet. You download the approved banners and upload them the way you do today.
