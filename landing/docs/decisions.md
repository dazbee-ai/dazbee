No more CSS 3D: the ground is one plane sheared into 2:1 iso (`.t-ground`), and buildings are sprites on lots of exactly their footprint, stacked back to front by their nearest corner. Grid: cell = 64 world px = 128 x 64 screen diamond; map 80 x 80 cells, repeating every 10 (road, pavement, 7 lot cells, pavement). Block plans (houses, shops, flats, park) are fixed layouts with random picks of same-size buildings. `tools/fit-sprites.py` replaces cut-sprites.py: it finds each image's slab edges and warps it onto the grid (each side of the nearest corner gets its own scale and shear, walls stay upright), and flags images that need too much correction. The four banner ads are painted onto the blank panels of the shop row roof board, bus shelter poster, office tower wall and wide billboard (PANELS in town.js, in sprite px).
