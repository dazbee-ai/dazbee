## 3. The problem
Ten near-identical versions of one ad barely move results anymore. Meta's ad system rewards ideas that are actually different, and every new idea means another round of building, resizing and approval.

1. Bring the brief. In your own format, with the client's brand saved once.
2. Get distinct routes. Different angles on your strategy, each an editable banner in every size.
3. Get the yes. One review link for the client. Only approved banners go out.
