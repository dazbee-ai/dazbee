# Agents' ads: the 10 briefs

The briefs of the before and after runs of the spec "agents make professional ads through the MCP server" (#123). Each run gives headless Claude Code one brief, word for word, followed by the line under "Every brief ends with". Every run works in the brand named Ikea; the products, people and events are made up. Keep the words unchanged so the before and after runs compare.

`run.py` reads the text inside each brief's fence; `<set>` becomes `before` or `after` in the design's name.

## Every brief ends with

```text
Work in the Dazbee brand named Ikea (the only brand you can see). Don't ask me questions; make sensible choices yourself and finish the job.
```

## 1. Sale

```text
Our end-of-season sale starts Friday: up to 50% off outdoor furniture, only until Sunday 30 November. I need an Instagram feed post, 1080x1080, that makes people stop scrolling, with a "Shop the sale" button. Name the design 'MCP <set> – Outdoor sale'.
```

## 2. Product launch

```text
We're launching GLÖDA, a smart table lamp that changes colour with the time of day and is controlled from your phone. It arrives in stores on 15 December at ₹3,499. Make a 1080x1920 Instagram Story that announces the launch and builds a bit of excitement. Name the design 'MCP <set> – GLÖDA launch'.
```

## 3. Single product

```text
Facebook link ad, 1200x628, for one product: the STRÅLA dining chair. Solid oak, stackable, ₹4,990. Keep it clean and let the chair be the hero, with a "Shop now" button. Name the design 'MCP <set> – STRÅLA chair'.
```

## 4. Lifestyle

```text
I want a lifestyle ad for our bedroom textiles, 1080x1350 for the Instagram feed. Theme: slow Sunday mornings at home. It should feel warm and calm, more mood than hard sell; a short line like "Make every morning a Sunday" and a small "Explore bedroom textiles" link is enough. Name the design 'MCP <set> – Slow Sundays'.
```

## 5. Testimonial

```text
Make a customer review ad, 1080x1080, around this quote: "Put it together in 20 minutes and it still feels rock solid three years later." – Priya S., Pune, about our TOSTERUP bookcase. Show that it is rated 4.8 out of 5 from 2,300 reviews. Name the design 'MCP <set> – TOSTERUP review'.
```

## 6. Event

```text
We're running a free workshop in store: "Small space, big ideas", where our designers show how to make a one-room flat work. Saturday 6 December, 11:00 to 13:00, at our Whitefield store in Bengaluru. Places are limited, people register on our website. I need an Instagram Story, 1080x1920. Name the design 'MCP <set> – Small space workshop'.
```

## 7. App download

```text
Ad to get people to download our app, Home Planner: plan your room in 3D, see furniture in your own space, and save your shopping list. Free on the App Store and Google Play. Make it 1080x1080 for Instagram and Facebook. Name the design 'MCP <set> – Home Planner app'.
```

## 8. Big number

```text
We want to shout about our returns promise: 365 days to change your mind, free returns on everything. The number should be the star of the ad. Size 1080x1080. Name the design 'MCP <set> – 365 days returns'.
```

## 9. Words only

```text
A words-only ad, no photos: "Small home? Big ideas." with the line "Storage that fits the way you live." and our name. It should still look designed, not like a plain text post. 1080x1350. Name the design 'MCP <set> – Small home big ideas'.
```

## 10. A set of sizes

```text
Back-to-school campaign for the PLUGGA study desk: compact, with a built-in cable tray, ₹7,990, "Study corner, sorted." I need it in four sizes: 1080x1080 (feed), 1080x1920 (Stories), 1200x628 (Facebook link ad) and 300x250 (web banner). Every size should look right on its own. Name the design 'MCP <set> – PLUGGA desk set'.
```
